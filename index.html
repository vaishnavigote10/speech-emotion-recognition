Important: the classifier is trained on synthetic feature distributions that approximate typical emotional speech (for example, anger has high energy and pitch; sadness is quiet and low). It has not been trained on real recordings such as RAVDESS, TESS, or EMO-DB, and microphone features are not calibrated to those datasets. Treat live predictions as a demonstration of the pipeline, not a reliable emotion detector. Accuracy will be much lower on real speech, and no model reaches 100%.
